How Furniture Crafting Works
• Craft at the Worktable (free from Welcome to Palia quest, or buy from Blacksmith for 200g).
• Recipes unlocked via Guild Store (Tish in Kilima), Inspiration (first-time craft set items), or event/quest rewards.
• Materials pulled from Inventory + General Storage (not Safe Storage).
• Inspiration: crafting a new set item can reveal up to 3 mystery recipes per set.
• Guild store requires Furniture Making skill levels 2–9 and costs 25–1,000 gold.
