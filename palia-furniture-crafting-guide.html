💡 How Furniture Crafting Works
• Craft at the Worktable (free from Welcome to Palia quest, or buy from Blacksmith for 200g).
• Recipes are unlocked by purchasing from the Furniture Making Guild Store (Tish in Kilima), from Inspiration (craft set items for the first time), or from event/quest rewards.
• All materials are pulled from your Inventory and General Storage (not Safe Storage).
• Each first-time craft of a set item can grant Inspiration for one of up to 3 mystery recipes in that set.
• Guild store recipes require Furniture Making skill levels 2–9 and cost 25–1,000 gold.
