💡 Pro Tips

  • Spice Sprouts — Found near Mirror Fields and Leafhopper Hills in Kilima Valley. Look west of the village.
  • Dari Cloves — Check Flooded Steps, Thorny Thicket, and Statue Garden in northern Bahari Bay. Only 1 spawns per server at a time.
  • Black Pearl (390g) — Elderwood shallow water. Check Mauvais Way and De Mer Dock.
  • Honey — Hangs on trees in Bahari Bay AND on Pine Trees in Mitana Grove (Elderwood).
  • Flow items — Flow Birch/Juniper/Oak/Pine appear during Flow events. Gather for rare seeds.
  • Kopaa Nuts — Hanging from Adult and Mature Ancient Trees in the Elderwood.
