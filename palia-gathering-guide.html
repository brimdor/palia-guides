💡 Pro Tips for Gatherers
• Spice Sprouts are found in Kilima Valley — look around the grassy areas near the river and village outskirts.
• Flow items (Flow Acorn, Flow Samara, Flow Pinecone, Flow Juniper Seed) are rare spawns that appear during Flow events or specific conditions — keep an eye out for glowing auras on gathering nodes.
• Housing Plot trees come in stages: Sapling → Young → Mature. Each stage has different sell values and some can be harvested for wood.
• Elderwood contains the most Epic-tier forageables (Black Pearl, Elderflower) — bring a large inventory.
• Bahari Bay has the widest variety of gatherables including shells, pearls, coral, and beach flowers.
• Wild Garlic, Wild Ginger, and Spice Sprouts are essential cooking ingredients — stock up when you find them.
• Many flowers appear in multiple zones — if a zone is crowded, try another for the same item.
