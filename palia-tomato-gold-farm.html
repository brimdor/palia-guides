Step 2 — Plant & Tend
Planting the Farm (Day 1)
  1. Place all 9 soil tiles on your housing plot, arranged in a connected grid
  2. Till each soil square with your Hoe
  3. Plant Tomato Plant Seeds in all 9 squares (9 × 80🪙 = 720🪙)
  4. Apply Harvest Boost Fertilizer to every square — this is critical, it increases yield from 2→3 per harvest
  5. Water every square daily. Tomatoes have Water Retain buff — they hydrate adjacent crops, but you must still water Day 1

Daily Routine
  1. Water all plants — even with Water Retain, always check. Missed water = missed growth day
  2. Reapply Harvest Boost Fertilizer if consumed (1 per growth cycle per square)
  3. Harvest any ready tomatoes — 3 per plant with Harvest Boost!
  4. Feed tomatoes into Preserves Jar (1 tomato = 1 Pickled Tomato, 27 min)
  5. Feed 3 tomatoes into Seed Collector to make 2 new seeds (30 min) — keeps the cycle self-sustaining
💧 Water Retain Synergy: Tomatoes' Water Retain buff hydrates adjacent crops. Plant in a connected grid so they keep each other hydrated — this reduces (but doesn't eliminate) the need for daily watering. Always verify they're watered!

Preserves Jar Breakdown
  1. Place Preserves Jar on your housing plot (craft at Worktable, recipe from Gardening Guild at Lv8)
  2. Crafting: Heartwood Plank ×16 + Iron Bar ×2 + Fabric ×6
  3. Load tomatoes into the jar — each becomes 1 Pickled Tomato in 27 real minutes
  4. Sell Pickled Tomatoes via Shipping Bin on your plot
  5. Place multiple jars to process in parallel — each jar runs independently
🫙 More Jars = More Gold: Each Preserves Jar processes 1 tomato at a time. For 9 plots producing ~108 tomatoes per cycle, you'll want 3–4 jars minimum to keep up with production. Each jar costs the same recipe — you just craft more at the Worktable.

Step 4 — Self-Sustaining Cycle
Never Buy Seeds Again
  1. Unlock the Seed Collector from Gardening Guild at Gardening Lv5 (1,000🪙 recipe)
  2. Craft at Worktable: Sapwood Plank ×16 + Copper Bar ×2 + Ceramic ×6 + Plant Fiber ×10
  3. Place on plot — it automatically collects seeds from fully-grown crops nearby
  4. Or manually feed: 3 Tomatoes → 2 Seeds (30 min craft time)
  5. Each plant yields 12 tomatoes (4 harvests × 3 with boost). Sacrifice 3 tomatoes for 2 seeds — that's 9 tomatoes profit per plant after seed replacement
🔄 The Loop: Harvest tomatoes → ~3 per plant go to Seed Collector → ~9 per plant go to Preserves Jar → Sell pickled tomatoes → Replant seeds from collector. Once set up, the farm runs on itself.

Pro Tips & Optimizations
Maximizing Yield
  1. Always use Harvest Boost Fertilizer — the +50% yield (2→3 per harvest) is the single biggest gold multiplier
  2. Stack fertilizer up to 99 per square — set it and forget it for the full cycle
  3. Daily watering is non-negotiable — missed water = missed growth day = delayed harvest
  4. Tomatoes' Water Retain helps neighbors but doesn't replace your own watering — always double-check
  5. Place Preserves Jars near the garden for quick loading — jars pull from storage automatically if connected
Getting ⭐ Star Quality
  1. Star Quality tomatoes sell for 50% more — 34🪙 raw, 51🪙 pickled
  2. Higher gardening skill = higher star quality chance
  3. Quality Up Fertilizer can boost star chance further — use it if you have surplus
  4. Star quality carries through preserving — ⭐ tomato → ⭐ pickled tomato
Scaling Beyond 9 Plots
  1. 9 soil is the max per player — but you can farm on multiple housing plots
  2. Each plot can hold its own garden + Preserves Jars
  3. Stagger planting across plots so harvests don't all land on the same day
  4. Automate seed production with Seed Collectors on each plot
Leveling Gardening Fast
  1. Gardening Lv5 — Unlocks Seed Collector (self-sustaining seeds)
  2. Gardening Lv8 — Unlocks Preserves Jar (the big gold multiplier)
  3. Every harvest, watering, and fert application gives Gardening XP — the farm levels itself
  4. Focus on getting to Lv8 ASAP — that's where the gold really starts flowing
