🔥 New Cooking Method: Campfire Recipes are a brand new cooking system in the Royal Highlands. Cook directly at Campfires found at campsites throughout the zone — no need to return home for simple, Focus-efficient meals.

💡 Key Design: Campfire recipes give you flexibility — cook while staying out in the world and continuing your adventure. They're NOT meant to replace home cooking. Use them for convenient Focus sustain, and save your Preserves Jar items for selling or higher-value recipes.

🧀 Cheese — New Rare Drop

Cheese is now an extremely rare drop obtainable from various activities throughout the Royal Highlands. It's used as an ingredient in some Campfire recipes.

⚠️ Focus Balance Change

The devs fixed an issue where Certain Preserves Jar items granted more Focus than intended. The new Campfire recipes are designed to fill that Focus need, freeing up your Preserves Jar items for selling or other uses.

Campsites in the Royal Highlands

Campsites are scattered across the Royal Highlands. Each real-world day, two campsites become active for you to discover.

Each active campsite includes:

  • 🏕️ A Campfire for cooking Campfire recipes
  • 📦 A Traveler's Cache with useful rewards
  • 🔍 Rummage Piles — daily opportunity to find artifact pieces and other finds

Once you find and collect from a cache, that campsite is marked on your map permanently, making it easy to return if it becomes active again on another day.
