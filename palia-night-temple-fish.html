💡 Quick Tips
• Glow Worm is the most needed bait — stock up by digging at night
• Fine Rod or better is recommended for all rare/epic fish (cave fish, stingrays, rays)
• Standard Rod works for: Shimmerfin, Radiant Sunfish, Striped Sturgeon, Dawnray, Rainbow Trout
• Bahari Bay Caves are the hotspot for the hardest fish (Mutated Angler, Void Ray, Stonefish, Hypnotic Moray)
• Time-gated fish: Dawnray (dawn only), Long Nosed Unicorn Fish (day only), Stormray (dawn/evening), Flametongue Ray (dawn/day)
• Giant Kilima Stingray is available most of the day (dawn/day/evening) but requires Glow Worm
