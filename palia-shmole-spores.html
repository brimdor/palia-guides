🍄 Shmole Spores

Elderwood Hunting Guide

⚠️
Not in Bahari Bay

Shmoles and Shmole Spores are exclusive to the Elderwood region — not Bahari Bay. The Elderwood is its own zone, accessed separately from Kilima and Bahari.

📍

Where to Find Shmoles

All three Shmole variants share the same caves in the Elderwood:

🌹 Red Blossom Cave
Elderwood
💜 Lilac Cavern
Elderwood
🌊 Vieuxport Sea
Elderwood

Shmoles live in tunnel networks called Shmole Holes — dirt mounds on floors, walls, and ceilings. They never leave the tunnels. When spooked, they dive and emerge from a different hole.

💡 Quick Tips

  • Shmoles are in caves (not forests or open areas) — head to Red Blossom Cave, Lilac Cavern, or Vieuxport Sea
  • Look for dirt mounds on walls and ceilings — those are Shmole Holes
  • Shmole Spores are a random drop — you may need to hunt several to get one
  • Piksii Shmole is much rarer than the common variant
  • All Shmoles are active anytime — no time restrictions
  • Infected variants drop Infected Essence (use Elderwood Arrows or Bag of Illusion IV)
  • Collecting all 3 spore types completes the Whack-a-Shmole! accomplishment
  • Shmole Spore is used in the daily quest Spore Me and the guide quest Home and Back Again
