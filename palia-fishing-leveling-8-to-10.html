Optimal Grinding Strategy
  • 1 Housing Plot Ponds + Glow Worms — the single best grind if you have Glow Worms. Fish your own ponds 24/7 with no travel time. Giant Goldfish (946 XP with focus) is the holy grail, but even the common Glow Worm pond fish (Crucian Carp 82★, Fathead Minnow 82★, Chub 182★, Kenli's Carp 398★) outpace worm/no-bait fish elsewhere. Zero downtime, zero time restrictions.
    ~8 perfect Giant Goldfish = Level 9 | ~11 more = Level 10
  • 2 Bahari Coast + Worms — Blue Marlin (326 XP with focus) is your best worm fish, available anytime. Also snag Long Nosed Unicorn Fish (Glow Worm, Day) if you're there during daytime. Combine with nearby river spots for Energized Piranha / Thundering Eel.
  • 3 Cave Runs + Glow Worms — Void Ray (872 XP with focus) and Swordfin Eel (872 XP) are both available anytime. Bahari caves also spawn Mutated Angler (326 XP, worm). Caves have fewer fish types so your Glow Worm is more likely to land an Epic.
  • 4 Always aim for Perfect Catch — that +20% stacks with focus bonus. If rod health is full when you land the fish, you get the Perfect bonus. Use Fish Stew or Fisherman's Brew to make this easier.
  • 5 Keep Focus at 100% at all times — eat food that restores focus between catches. At 100% focus, you literally double your XP. Don't fish with empty focus.
  • 6 Hit Level 9 → Craft Exquisite Rod immediately — then the remaining 5,000 XP to level 10 will feel much easier with the best rod in the game.

💡 Quick Tips
• Glow Worms are gated behind Fishing Lv10 (Glow Worm Farm or Fishing Guild purchase). If you don't have a stash, use Worms for the Blue Marlin grind at Bahari Coast — 326 XP per perfect catch at 100% focus, ~12 perfect catches for 8→9
• Party fishing gives bonus chance at double catches — fish near friends if possible
• Star quality fish give ~2× the XP of regular. Fish from hot spots (rippling water nodes) for guaranteed star quality
• Craft Fisherman's Brew (you unlocked it at Lv8!) — any fish + spice + wild garlic. Makes perfect catches much more consistent
• No-bait fishing is very slow for leveling (72 XP max per catch with focus). Only do it if you're totally out of bait and desperate
• Once you hit Lv9, the Exquisite Rod + Major Safe Zone Booster makes the 9→10 grind feel dramatically easier
• Theoretical minimum: At 946 XP per Giant Goldfish, you need just ~4 for 8→9 and ~6 for 9→10. That's 10 perfect catches total! Realistically plan for 30-50 catches depending on your Glow Worm supply and luck
