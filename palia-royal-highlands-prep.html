1. Stockpile Gold
  • Hit 300k–500k gold before May 12 The Handler's License (to ride bred horses), new recipes, decor, Jadium storage, and 3rd-story upgrade will all demand gold. Expect 50k+ for the license alone.
  • Maximize passive income: Apple Jam 144g/jar Your 2 apple trees + preserve jars are the best AFK gold. One full shipping bin of Star Apple Jam ≈ 103,000g. Keep jars running 24/7.
  • Run Blueberry Jam as secondary 88g/jar Second-best preserve jar product. Fill any empty jar slots with blueberry jam or pickled tomatoes (51g).
  • Do a Cake Party before launch 80k+/hr Best active gold in the game. Coordinate via Palia Discord LFG. One session can bank 100k+ in an hour.
  • Use Seed Maker for potatoes/cotton High value Star Potato Seeds and Star Cotton Seeds sell well. Seed makers should run on potatoes/cotton while preserve jars handle fruits.
🪵
2. Gather Building Materials
  • Planks & Stone Bricks Bulk The Ancient Human Home Line + 3rd story upgrade will be a massive material sink. Stockpile hundreds of each tier: Sapwood, Heartwood, Flow, and Stone Bricks.
  • Fabric & Leather New decor, clothing dye system, and potentially saddle/crafting recipes may need cloth. Farm Sernuk for leather and cotton for fabric.
  • Palium Bars & Flow Planks Epic tier Jadium storage chests and high-end housing upgrades will likely require the current top-tier materials. Mine Palium nodes in Bahari Bay and Elderwood caves daily.
  • Standard Arrows New creatures to hunt in the Highlands = more arrow consumption. Craft a few hundred now so you can hunt immediately on arrival.
🐴
3. Prep Your Ranch for Horses
  • Complete quest "Like a Bird" Required If you haven't unlocked Ranching yet, this quest from Delaila is the gate. You need the Ranch to breed or stable horses.
  • Level Ranching to at least 5 North Pen Ranching Lv5 unlocks the North (Water) Pen — 10 more animal slots. More pens = more room for horses + existing animals.
  • Push to Ranching 8 if possible East Pen Ranching Lv8 unlocks the East (Fire) Pen. That's 40 total animal slots across all pens — ideal for a full horse breeding operation.
  • Clear 2–4 animal slots Horses will need pen space. Consider donating older animals (elders/venerated) or consolidating species. Remember: babies and venerated animals don't produce items.
  • Stockpile animal feed & treats Horses will have happiness/focus mechanics just like other ranch animals. Keep feed ready so new horses hit max productivity fast.
  • Save gold for Handler's License Bred horses can't leave your Ranch as mounts without this license. It's purchased with gold — expect a steep price (50k+ speculated).

4. Prepare Your Housing Plot
  • Plan for the Ancient Human Home Line This is a new elevated plot style, not just a furniture set. It may require clearing or relocating existing structures. Scout your current plot for open space.
  • Save for the 3rd Story Upgrade First-ever third-floor housing expansion. This will cost significant gold and materials. Don't blow your savings on decor before launch.
  • Clear plot clutter New Aristocrat's Decor (Rococo), Teatime sets, and 10 Fancypods mean you'll want clean space to showcase them. Store unused decor in chests now.

5. Maximize Storage
  • Place all 8 general + 3 lockbox chests Current max is 8 general chests (Wooden→Palium) + 3 lockboxes. That's 14,300 slots total. Make sure yours are all placed — you can have 8 per plot.
  • Upgrade to Palium chests 1,750 slots each Recipe costs 100,000g from Tish's Material Store. If you haven't bought it yet, do so before launch. 8 Palium chests = 14,000 general slots.
  • Free up inventory space A new zone means new gatherables, fish, bugs, and minerals. Don't hit launch day with a full backpack. Dump excess into chests or sell low-priority stacks.
  • Save gold for Jadium tier New tier Jadium is the new storage tier above Palium. It will likely cost 150k–250k+ gold for the recipe and require new Highlands materials to craft.
