General Tips
Pro tips for completing all temple bundles efficiently
🎣 Fishing Tips
Stock up on Worms and Glow Worms before bundle fishing sessions Fine Rod or Exquisite Rod is strongly recommended for Epic fish Time-gated fish: plan sessions around Dawn (6-8am), Evening (5-8pm), and Night (8pm-6am) Cave fish need Glow Worm bait and are found in specific cave zones Each in-game day ≈ 1.5 real hours — use time efficiently
🦋 Bug Catching Tips
Always carry Smoke Bombs — craft at Worktable or buy from Bug Catching Guild Honey Lures attract specific bug types to your position for easier catching Crouch-approach running bugs (crabs, beetles) before throwing Smoke Bomb Flying bugs (moths, dragonflies) — throw Smoke Bomb ahead of their flight path Many bundle bugs are Evening/Night only — plan accordingly Epic bugs (Jewelwing Dragonfly, Ancient Amber Beetle) may take many attempts
🍳 Cooking Tips
Check your recipe book — many bundle recipes unlock from Guild Stores or friendship quests Some recipes require ingredients from other skills (fish, foraged items) Cooking gives Focus buffs — use them while gathering bundle items! Apple Pie & Blueberry Pie: Grow Apples and Blueberries in your garden first Chapaa Masala: Requires Muujin Meat from hunting + Heat Root from foraging Muujin Bahari: Multiple skill gates — hunting for Muujin + cooking skills needed
⛏️ Mining & Foraging Tips
Emerald & Jasper: Epic starstones — random rare drops from mining nodes Emerald from Palium nodes (Elderwood), Jasper from any node (rare) Gold Bars: Mine Gold Ore (Bahari) → Smelt at Basic Smelter Palium Bars: Mine Palium Ore (Elderwood) → Smelt — need 20 for Roots bundle! Heat Root: Rare forage in Bahari Bay — need 5 for Flames bundle Brightshroom: Rare forage in Bahari Bay — need 20 for Roots bundle! Dari Cloves: Epic forage in Bahari Bay — very rare, be patient Green Pearl: Epic forage in Bahari Bay — rare beach find
🔍 Exploring Medallions
Each temple has an exploring medallion bundle (6 medallions each) Medallions are found as glowing pickups in the overworld near their temple zone Emberseeker Medallions: Found around the Flames temple area Stormseeker Medallions: Found around the Gales temple area Rootseeker Medallions: Found around the Roots temple area Medallions respawn — check back daily if you haven't found all 6
🌱 Gardening Tips
Apples (×20 for Roots bundle): Plant Apple Seeds → harvest over multiple days Apple seeds available from General Store or Gardening Guild Use Quality Fertilizer to improve Apple yield per harvest Plan ahead — garden crops take real-time hours to grow!
