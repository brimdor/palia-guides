Crop Buff System

Each crop type emits a passive buff to adjacent (non-diagonal) crops of different types. Buffs do not affect same-type neighbors and do not stack with fertilizer — but they do stack with each other (a crop can receive multiple adjacent buffs).

💧 Water Retain — Adjacent crops stay watered longer (less watering)
🌾 Harvest Boost — Adjacent crops yield +50% more on harvest
⭐ Quality Boost — Adjacent crops drop more Star Quality items
🌿 Weed Block — Weeds cannot grow on adjacent tiles

Processing Priority (Preserve Jars & Seed Maker)

Your bottleneck is processing capacity, not growing capacity. With limited preserve jars, process in this order:

  1. Apple Jam — 144g/jar. Always run first. 12 apples every 4 days = 12 jar runs.
  2. Blueberry Jam — 88g/jar. Second priority. ~6 berries every 4 days = 6 jar runs.
  3. Pickled Tomatoes — 51g/jar. Fill empty slots. ~24 tomatoes per 10-day cycle = 24 jar runs.
  4. Pickled Corn — 60g/jar. Use spare capacity while apples/berries/tomatoes are processing.
  5. Seed Maker: Cotton & Potato — Run overnight or when preserve jars are full. Star seeds sell for ~120g.
💡 Pro Tip: Place your Preserve Jars and Seed Maker inside your house (not outside) so crafters can access materials directly from storage chests. This eliminates inventory micromanagement.
🧪
Fertilizer Strategy

Apply one fertilizer per tile per day of the crop's growth cycle. Partial fertilization = proportional chance (e.g., 4 days of fertilizer on a 5-day potato = 80% chance of bonus).

FertilizerEffectBest Used On
QualityUpMore Star Quality itemsApple Trees, Blueberries (preserve jar crops)
HarvestBoost+50% yield countCotton, Potato (seed maker crops), Corn
SpeedyGroGrowth time −1 daySlow crops: Potato, Corn, Cotton (5-day crops)
⚠️ Important: Adjacent crop buffs do not stack with fertilizer of the same type. But they DO stack with different buffs. A cotton center getting Water Retain from tomatoes + Harvest Boost from corn will benefit from both simultaneously.

Watering Schedule

With the Water Retain adjacency from Tomatoes and Potatoes, your watering workload drops significantly.

  • Apple Trees: Water once after planting; trees need minimal ongoing water
  • Blueberry + Tomato plots: Tomatoes give Water Retain to blueberries and corn = water every 2 days instead of daily
  • Tomato-Cotton-Corn plots: 4 Tomatoes per plot give Water Retain to the center cotton and surrounding corn = water every 2 days for most tiles
  • Potato-Tomato flex plot: Checkerboard means every tile is adjacent to a tomato = uniform Water Retain coverage
⏰ Time Saver:>/strong> Group your watered-together plots close to each other on your housing plot. A single watering can pass can hit multiple tiles. Apple trees at the far edge (minimal water); synergy grids clustered near your house.
