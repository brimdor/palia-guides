The Pulsewater Plains fast travel area in Bahari Bay is the top Honey Lure spot. The Fairy Mantis (Epic, 660✦) spawns here anytime.

During Dawn/Day, stand near the cliffs border toward Pavel Mines for a chance at the Rainbow-tipped Butterfly (Epic, 660✦) — two Epics from one lure!

🍯 Honey Lure Tips

Duration: Lasts 3 minutes — have your net ready before placing!

Cost: 75 Bug Catching tokens at the Guild Store (Level 10 to purchase, Level 7 for recipe)

Craft: 1× Gold Bar + 1× Dragon's Beard Peat + 4× Apple at Worktable

Best move: Pulsewater Plains near the Pavel Mines cliff border during Dawn/Day = 2× Epic bugs possible

Night shift: Evening/Night in Pulsewater Plains adds Bahari Glowbug (Rare, 217✦)

Sell tip: Always sell at star quality for the ✦ values shown
