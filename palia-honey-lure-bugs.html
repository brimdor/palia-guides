💡 Pro Tips
• Always party up — more players = more rare spawns. This is the single biggest factor
• Use smoke candles + sneaky/smoke bombs — regular bombs scare bugs away and scatter them
• Catch common bugs too — they occupy spawn slots; clearing them makes room for rares to appear
• Pair with a Buzzy Jar for rare spawn pings during your lure
• Arrive early: Dawn/early morning is the best window for #1 and #2 locations
• For Gossamer Veil Moths specifically, be at Windmill Fields by 5:45 PM in-game, pop a Buzzy Jar at 6 PM
• Bring 80+ smoke bombs per lure session — don't run out mid-catch
🤝 Community Etiquette
• Call out in server chat when you place a lure — others will join and that benefits everyone
• Ask before joining someone else's lure — they spent gold bars to craft it
• For Rainbow Tipped Butterfly plush hunts: agree on rules first (catch only butterflies, not grasshoppers/mantis)
• Honey lures can cause lag on Switch — be mindful of platform mix in groups
• Bug sharing is inconsistent — party up to ensure you both get credit
