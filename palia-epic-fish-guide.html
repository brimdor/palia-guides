Epic Fish Guide

Complete equipment, bait, locations & loadout for all 9 Epic fish

🪱 The Rod: Exquisite Rod Best-in-Slot

The highest tier rod — Epic rarity, 3,000 durability, fastest reel speed, most line health.

Upgrade Path

Makeshift Rod (free) → Standard Rod (Lvl 3, 200g) → Fine Rod (Lvl 6, 1,500g) → Exquisite Rod (Lvl 9, 3,000g)

Crafting Recipe

5× Flow-Infused Plank + 5× Palium Bar + 1× Fine Rod → at Worktable

Key insight: Rod quality does NOT increase the probability of an Epic fish appearing — that's determined by location + time + bait. But a better rod makes the minigame dramatically easier when an Epic does hook, since Epics have very aggressive movement patterns.

⚙️ Attachment & Bait

Attachment: Major Safe Zone Size Booster

The single most impactful attachment. Enlarges the green catch zone during the minigame — critical for the erratic movement of Epic fish.

5 Fishing Medals • Requires: Fishing Lvl 10 • Uses: 25 before breaking

Secondary option: Major Rod Recovery Booster — great if struggling with line health on long fights.

Bait: Glow Worm Non-Negotiable

Every single Epic fish requires Glow Worms. Regular Worms will not attract them.

10 Fishing Medals → 20 Glow Worms or 1 Worm + 1 Glow Berry → Glow Worm Farm

5 of 9 Epics are catchable Anytime — no time-gating needed.

Flametongue Ray has the tightest window (Morning & Day only).

No weather conditions affect any Epic fish — go whenever you have Glow Worms.
