How to Get Each Chest
All recipes from Tish's Furniture Store → Material Store (back register)
🪵 Wooden Storage Chest
  1. Recipe is free — granted during the Welcome to Palia intro quest
  2. Craft at the Worktable with: Sapwood ×20 + Flint ×2
  3. Sapwood: Chop trees in Kilima Valley (any tree, basic axe)
  4. Flint: Forage from rocks on the ground in Kilima Valley
🟤 Copper Storage Chest
  1. Buy recipe at Tish's Furniture Store (Material Store) for 10,000🪙
  2. Craft at the Worktable with: Wooden Chest ×1 + Sapwood Plank ×20 + Copper Bar ×3
  3. Sapwood Plank: Process Sapwood at a Sawmill (1 min each) — Foraging Lv2
  4. Copper Bar: Smelt Copper Ore ×5 at a Smelter (3 min each) — Mining Guild recipe: 25🪙
  5. Copper Ore: Mine copper nodes in Kilima Valley caves and mines
⚪ Iron Storage Chest
  1. Buy recipe at Tish's Furniture Store (Material Store) for 50,000🪙
  2. Craft at the Worktable with: Copper Chest ×1 + Heartwood Plank ×20 + Iron Bar ×3
  3. Heartwood Plank: Process Heartwood at a Sawmill (2 min each) — Foraging Lv4, recipe: 500🪙
  4. Heartwood: Chop trees in Bahari Bay (needs Standard Axe+)
  5. Iron Bar: Smelt Iron Ore ×5 at a Smelter (6 min each) — Mining Guild recipe: 100🪙
  6. Iron Ore: Mine iron nodes in Bahari Bay caves and mines
🔵 Palium Storage Chest
  1. Buy recipe at Tish's Furniture Store (Material Store) for 100,000🪙
  2. Craft at the Worktable with: Iron Chest ×1 + Ancient Wood Plank ×20 + Palium Bar ×3
  3. Ancient Wood Plank: Process Ancient Wood at a Sawmill (3 min each) — Foraging Lv5, recipe: 500🪙
  4. Ancient Wood: Chop ancient trees in Elderwood zone
  5. Palium Bar: Smelt Palium Ore ×5 at a Smelter (12 min each!) — Mining Lv6, recipe: 500🪙
  6. Palium Ore: Mine in Elderwood — requires Fine Pick or Exquisite Pick. Also from Rummage Piles & Temple of the Roots chests
🔒 Lockbox Storage Chest
  1. Buy recipe at Tish's Furniture Store (Material Store) for 1,000🪙
  2. Craft at the Worktable with: Sapwood Plank ×10 + Copper Bar ×2
  3. Materials are easy — same sources as Copper Chest ingredients

How Storage Works
Key Mechanics
RuleDetail
All chests connected Contents shared across all chests on all plots — access from any chest
Crafter access Crafters can pull from general storage automatically (not lockboxes)
Lockbox protection Items in lockboxes are safe from being auto-consumed by crafters
Multi-plot storage 8 general chests per plot. Chests on other plots give access only — not extra slots
Chests upgrade in-place Each tier requires the previous chest as an ingredient — you don't lose your placed chest count
💡 Storage Tips
• You can't go beyond 8 general chests — upgrade existing ones instead of adding more
• Upgrading all 8 from Wooden → Palium: 3,200 → 14,000 slots (+10,800!)
• Best value first upgrade: Wooden → Copper — cheapest at 10K gold, +425 slots each
• Each upgrade requires the previous chest as an ingredient, so you keep your same 8 slots
• Palium Bars take 12 minutes to smelt — plan ahead and batch smelt
• Lockboxes aren't for bulk storage — use them to protect valuables from crafters
• Iron recipe was reduced from 100K → 50K gold — Palium may drop in future updates
