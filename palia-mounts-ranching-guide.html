🐴 Mounts & Ranching Guide

Royal Highlands Expansion — Build 0.202
Everything you need to know about horses, riding, breeding, and ranching
🐎 New Feature: Mounts have arrived in Palia! For the first time, you can own, ride, and breed horses. The Royal Highlands expansion introduces an entire ranching system alongside the mount mechanic.

Getting Your First Horse

🐴 The Introductory Quest

When you first arrive in the Royal Highlands, you'll pick up a quest that introduces you to your first horse. This quest is Available Immediately — it's not tied to Main Story progression, so you can complete it regardless of where you are in your questing journey.

Quest reward: Your very first Dawnspur horse! This also unlocks horse ranching, allowing you to purchase, breed, and raise additional horses.

💡 Tip: Finish your Elderwood quests and adventures before heading to the Royal Highlands for the best experience. Access the Highlands via the De Mer Docks in the Elderwood — take the boat across.

Horse Breeds

🐴 Dawnspur
Your first companion — loyal and versatile
🐎 Inspired by: American Quarter Horse

The Dawnspur is the horse every player will meet first. Originally bred by the Mudan and once favored by Ancient Human royalty, it's a reliable, approachable mount perfect for exploring the Highlands.

Free — Quest Reward No Premium Required

How to get: Complete the introductory horse quest upon arriving in the Royal Highlands.

🐎 Akwindu Thoroughbred
The collector's breed — unlock through breeding mastery
🐎 Inspired by: Arabian

This elegant breed is unlocked by collecting a wide variety of horse traits through breeding. The more traits you discover through your breeding program, the closer you get to unlocking the Akwindu Thoroughbred.

Breeding Unlock Riding License (Supporter Pack)

Riding access: Requires a Riding License that comes exclusively in the Highlands Supporter Packs.

🔥 Phoenixfire Dawnspur
Mythical variant — touched by the Phoenix itself
🔥 Rarity: Mythical

Long thought to exist only in legend, the Phoenixfire Dawnspur is the rarest of mounts. Obtain it by progressing through Binnie's side quests in the Royal Highlands.

Mythical Riding License (Premium Store)

Riding access: Requires a Riding License purchased from the Premium Store.

⚠️ Riding License: Both the Akwindu Thoroughbred and Phoenixfire Dawnspur require a Riding License to actually ride them — even after you obtain the horse. The Dawnspur from the intro quest does not require a license for that specific horse. Licenses come from:
  • Highlands Supporter Packs (for Akwindu Thoroughbred)
  • Premium Store (for Phoenixfire Dawnspur)

⚡ Stamina System

Riding at top speed (sprint) consumes horse stamina. When stamina runs out, your horse automatically reduces to gallop speed. Stamina regenerates over time while riding at lower speeds.

Flow Sparks — Glowing energy orbs scattered across the Royal Highlands — instantly restore some of your horse's stamina when collected. Think of them as stamina pick-ups along your riding routes.

Riding Controls & Accessibility

🎮 Steering Modes
Camera follows horse by default. Options:
Camera-Follow (default) — Camera turns with your horse
Manual — You control camera independently
Full Auto — Camera handles everything
Hybrid — Mix of follow and manual
📋 Menu Behavior
Horses pause automatically when you open dialogue or the map. You can adjust this setting to keep your horse moving while interacting with menus if you prefer.
🏃 Speed Control
Tap up/down to finely adjust speed through gaits.
Hold up to move at maximum speed.
Hold S / hold down on left stick to stop quickly.
🦘 Jumping & Collision
Horses can jump over obstacles! If your horse hits an obstacle, it will buckle and stop. Plan your routes and use jumps to navigate the terrain.
💡 Where Can You Ride? Horses can be ridden in most locations, including your housing plot. Restricted areas: Village Center, Underground, and certain private spaces. No horsing around on the Hot Pot tables!

Stables — Fast Travel Network

🏛️ Stable System

Stables serve as the fast travel network in the Royal Highlands. They're scattered throughout the zone, but — like other discoverables — you must discover them first before you can use them for travel.

Once discovered, stables appear on your map and allow quick transit between locations across the Highlands.

Breeding & Ranching

🐴 The Ranch

The Ranching skill allows you to raise animals, collect their resources, and breed them. Start by completing the ranching quest, then Delaila will give you a recipe for a Ranch Warp item that teleports you to your personal ranch.

Your ranch includes:

  • Roaming areas for animals
  • A store for supplies and new animals
  • Storage access
  • A breeding pen with a reference book of "Daiya family wisdom"

Important: Animals at Baby or Venerated age do NOT produce items or breed.

Keeping too many of the same species can reduce production — diversify your ranch!

🏗️ Pen Organization

Pens hold up to 10 animals each. Use the permissions menu on the animal roster table to prioritize pen assignments.

Pen types: Any Pen • Air Pen • Water Pen • Fire Pen

Check the Ranch Map to confirm which pen is which.

Multiplayer Ranching

Visitors can come to your ranch and help care for your animals! They can pet, feed, and collect items from your animals.

How it works:

  • Visitors must travel to your Housing Plot and ring your doorbell
  • Experience from petting, feeding, and collecting is shared between visitor and owner
  • All collected items go to the owner's inventory
  • The hosting player must be present for visitors to gather produce
  • Visitors CAN feed and pet animals even if the host is on their housing plot
💡 XP Sharing: Only the interacting visitor and the owner gain experience. Other party members (not directly interacting) do NOT gain XP. If the host interacts, visitors gain no XP.

Horse Traits & Breeding

Horses can display a variety of visual and functional traits through breeding. Traits start with realistic coats inspired by real-life breeds and extend to rarer, more fantastical options.

Breeding mechanics:

  • Horses you breed can be ridden once fully grown
  • Younger ponies need time to mature before becoming rideable
  • The breeding pen on your ranch is where horses produce offspring
  • Collecting a wide variety of traits is key to unlocking the Akwindu Thoroughbred

Horse Customization

Customize your riding experience with various options:

  • Steering Mode — Camera-follow, manual, full auto, or hybrid
  • Menu Behavior — Auto-pause or keep riding when menus are open
  • Speed Control — Tap/hold for fine or maximum speed adjustments
  • Stamina UI — Toggle the on-screen stamina display
  • Premium tack and cosmetics — Available in the Premium Store and Supporter Packs

The Long Ride — Community Event

🏇 May 25 – June 1

The Long Ride is a limited-time community event where the community works together to rack up mount distance and collect Amber Echo.

Points formula: Mount distance × Amber Echo collected = your contribution. Shared milestones unlock rewards for everyone!
