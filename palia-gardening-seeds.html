✨ Garden Buff Types

Each crop gives a buff to adjacent crops of a different type. Buffs don't stack with fertilizer — choose one or the other. Crops only affect orthogonally adjacent tiles (not diagonal). 2x2 crops need 2 buffing neighbors; 3x3 crops need 3.

Water Retain
1×1: Potato • Napa Cabbage • Tomato
Effect: Adjacent crops stay watered — no need to water them daily
Harvest Boost
1×1: Wheat • Rice • Corn
2×2: Blueberries • Batterfly Beans
3×3: Apple
Effect: +50% extra crop yield on harvest (same as HarvestBoost Fertilizer)
Weed Block
1×1: Carrot • Onion • Bok Choy
Effect: Prevents knapweed from growing on adjacent crops
Quality Boost
1×1: Cotton
2×2: Spicy Pepper • Rockhopper Pumpkin
Effect: Increases chance of Star Quality crop on harvest
Growth Boost
Not available from any crop — only from SpeedyGro Fertilizer
Effect: For every 2 days of growth, grants 1 bonus day (≈1.5× speed)
No Buff
1×1: Lettuce
Effect: None — receives buffs from neighbors but gives none

♻️ Multi-Harvest Crops Explained

Multi-harvest crops have a format like 4 + 2 + 2 + 2 Days:

PhaseDaysWhat Happens
First Harvest4 daysPlant → grow for 4 days → harvest once
Regrowth 12 daysRegrows automatically → harvest again
Regrowth 22 daysRegrows → harvest again
Regrowth 32 daysRegrows → final harvest, plant dies

💡 Key Insight

Tomato = 4 harvests total over 10 days. Each harvest yields 2-3 tomatoes. That's 8-12 tomatoes from one 80g seed — much better profit per tile than single-harvest crops!

💡 Seed Collector Tips

  • 1×1 single-harvest crops are the most seed-efficient — 1 crop → 3-6 seeds (self-sustaining!)
  • Tomato takes 3 crops → 2 seeds, but each tomato plant produces 8-12 tomatoes over its lifetime — still very sustainable
  • Apple is the worst ratio (10 apples → 1 seed) — buy Apple seeds from Badruu at Gardening Lvl 8 instead
  • Star Quality crops → Star Quality seeds (same ratio, higher sell value on the crop you grow)
  • Max 30 fruit input + 1 processing stack; 3 × 30 seed output stacks

⚠️ Fertilizer vs. Crop Buffs

Buffs from adjacent crops DON'T stack with fertilizer. If a crop is already getting Water Retain from a neighboring Tomato, adding Water Retain Fertilizer won't help. Choose fertilizer that complements rather than duplicates your garden layout's natural buffs.

🗺️ Garden Layout Strategy

📐 Layout Principles

  • Buff chains matter! Place Water Retain crops (Tomato/Potato/Napa Cabbage) next to Harvest Boost crops (Wheat/Rice/Corn) so both benefit
  • Same-type crops don't buff each other — a Tomato next to another Tomato gives no buff
  • Diagonal doesn't count — only orthogonal (up/down/left/right) adjacency
  • 2×2 crops need 2 buffing neighbors and 3×3 crops need 3 — they can be on different sides
  • Weed Block is a quality-of-life buff — saves you from manually weeding. Great for early gardens
  • Water Retain is a time-saver — eliminates daily watering. Huge for 9-tile gardens
  • Harvest Boost is the profit buff — +50% yield means +50% gold and +50% Seed Collector material

🏆 The Golden Pairing

Water Retain ↔ Harvest Boost is the strongest combo. Water Retain crops (Tomato, Potato, Napa Cabbage) save you time by auto-watering, while Harvest Boost crops (Wheat, Rice, Corn) give +50% yield. Alternate them in rows or columns for maximum mutual benefit!
