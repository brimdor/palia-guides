The Enfield Family

Artifacts in the Royal Highlands are all tied to the influential Enfield family. Four main Enfield family members each have unique artifacts scattered throughout the world, revealing different aspects of their story, character, and history.

The Enfield family ruled the Royal Highlands for centuries before the Unification Wars. Now, with humans returning, the land is waking up — and their legacy is yours to discover.

Relics

⚔️ What Are Relics?

Relics in the Royal Highlands are acquired as you progress through the Main Story. You'll receive the first tier of all four relics, each tied to one of the four Enfields.

Once you've completed the Main Story, Eshelon will guide you in restoring your relics to their full potential, now up to Level 5.

⚠️ Zone-Locked: Royal Highlands relics only work in the Royal Highlands. Previous zone relics (Elderwood, etc.) will NOT work in this zone. Plan your loadout accordingly!

Artifacts

🔍 How Artifacts Work

Artifact pieces are scattered throughout the Royal Highlands. Here's how the system works:

  • Most artifact pieces can be found in rummage piles at campsites and throughout the world
  • Rummage pile artifacts are tradable with other players at Trading Stations in the Central Stables
  • Rare artifacts come from the Echo Store or treasure chests — these are NOT tradable
  • Completing an artifact rewards Certificates of Merit which can be used to acquire upgrades for your Relics

Vaults

🔐 What Are Vaults?

Vaults are hidden treasures left behind by the Enfields. There are 20 total Vaults in the Royal Highlands:

  • 16 world Vaults — scattered throughout the zone, findable by exploration
  • 4 quest Vaults — tied to story progression

Each Vault is aligned with one of four elements. To unlock it, you need a matching Flow Mask of the same element.

Discoverables

🗺️ Treasure Chests

16 treasure chests are hidden throughout the Royal Highlands. Unlike Vaults, these are already unlocked — you can open them immediately!

🏺 Amber Echo

Amber Echo is the primary currency of the Royal Highlands, similar to Flow from the Elderwood — but far more deeply embedded into the zone.

Key mechanics:

  • You'll need to progress through the Main Story before you can collect Amber Echo
  • Royal Amber crystals are scattered throughout the zone — break them with any tool (Pickaxe, Axe, or Bow)
  • Amber Echo can be gathered from nearly everything in the zone — it's a constant part of exploration
  • Breaking open Amber Echo Crystals is much more rewarding than passive gathering

Primary Currency Story-Gated Unlock

🏠 Aristocrat's Set Discovery

The Aristocrat's furniture set can be found as discoverable pieces out in the world. When you find a piece, its location is tracked on your map so you can return later even if you're not ready to unlock it yet.

Recipes come from quest rewards and purchases using Amber Echo. The set will continue expanding in future patches.

Artifact Progression

📈 How to Progress

  • Main Story → Receive your first tier of all four relics
  • Complete Main Story → Eshelon guides relic restoration
  • Upgrade Relics → Use Certificates of Merit from completed artifacts
  • Find Artifacts → Rummage piles (tradable) + Echo Store / treasure chests (non-tradable)
  • Unlock Vaults → Collect Flow Masks from masked creatures → open Vaults for rewards
