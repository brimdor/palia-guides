Travel to the De Mer Docks in the Elderwood and take the boat to begin your journey into the Royal Highlands.

💡 Tip
The developers recommend finishing your Elderwood quests and adventures first before making the journey, as the Royal Highlands main story picks up directly where the Elderwood leaves off.

🗺️ Zone Overview

We wanted every corner of this new zone to feel special and intentional, with its own personality and sense of place. Mounts ended up shaping a lot of that journey, with winding paths opening up into sweeping vistas. It's the kind of place where you might set out with a goal, and then find yourself forgetting it completely and simply take in the breathtaking beauty of the world. — S6 Jaddua

Long before the Palia you know, the Royal Highlands were a place of royalty and prominence. The Enfields built their legacy here, shaping a region that would later be abandoned in the wake of the Unification Wars. For over four centuries, no one — except a few unique creatures — has called it home.

Now you are among the first to return. Horses roam once more, and the land itself feels different. Even Echo does not behave quite the same — Amber Echo has claimed pieces of the Highlands, holding moments of the past in perfect stillness.

💎 Amber Echo

Amber Echo is the primary currency of the Royal Highlands — similar to Infected Essence from the Elderwood, but far more deeply embedded into the land itself.

  • You need to progress through the Main Story before you can collect Amber Echo.
  • Once unlocked, Amber Echo can be gathered from nearly everything in the zone — making it a constant part of exploration.
  • Royal Amber crystals are the physical manifestation found throughout the Highlands. They can encase mining nodes, trees, hunting creatures, and insects.
  • Amber Echo Crystals can be broken with your Pickaxe, Axe, or Bow & Arrows. Any tier of tool works.
  • Breaking open crystals and gathering what's encased inside is much more rewarding than passive gathering alone.

🏕️ Campsites

Campsites are scattered across the Royal Highlands. Each real-world day, two of them will be active for you to discover.

  • Each campsite includes a Campfire you can cook at
  • A Traveler's Cache that may contain useful rewards
  • Rummage piles offering daily artifact pieces and other finds
  • Once you find and collect from a cache, that campsite is marked on your map
  • New Campfire recipes are more approachable — ingredients pull directly from storage, no need to carry them
  • Finished food is reserved for you for a short time before becoming available to others
💡 Tip
Campfire recipes are designed for on-the-go cooking — strong focus value for cost, without needing to return home. They can be made at star quality but are not placeable.

📦 Discoverables

16 Treasure Chests

16 treasure chests are hidden throughout the Royal Highlands. These are already unlocked and ready to be claimed immediately — no special keys or masks needed.

20 Vaults

20 Vaults total — 16 scattered across the zone + 4 tied to quests. Each Vault is aligned with one of four elements and requires a matching Flow Mask to unlock. Any Vault you interact with is tracked on your map for later return.

ℹ️ Vault Elements
Each vault requires a Flow Mask of the same element to open. The four elemental types are: Pyroflow (Red), Hydroflow (Blue), Terraflow (Green), and Aeroflow (Yellow).

Eshelon Bahari arrives in style with a reputation that precedes him. As the first and only son of Duchess Kenyara Bahari, he was raised to succeed and has done little else. 🐴 Stables & Fast Travel

Stables serve as fast travel points throughout the Royal Highlands and connect back to other zones. However, stables must be discovered first before they can be used for travel.

💡 Tip
Explore thoroughly early on! Each stable you discover becomes a permanent fast travel hub, saving you long rides across the zone.

Horses & Mounts

Mounts are a brand-new traversal system introduced with the Royal Highlands. Your first horse quest is immediately available upon entering the zone — not tied to Main Story progression.

  • Dawnspur — Inspired by the American Quarter Horse, once favored by Ancient Human royalty and originally bred by the Mudan. Your first horse.
  • Akwindu Thoroughbred — Arabian-inspired breed, unlocked by collecting horse traits through breeding. Riding requires the Highlands Supporter Pack Riding License.
  • Phoenixfire Dawnspur — Mythical variant touched by the Phoenix itself. Obtained via Binnie's side quests, with riding unlocked via Premium Store Riding License.

Horses move through multiple gaits (walk, trot, canter, gallop, sprint). Riding at top speed uses stamina. Flow Sparks scattered across the Highlands restore horse stamina instantly.

⚗️ Flow Masks

Flow Masks are drops from hunting Masked Creatures (Jaakcats and Sernuk). Each mask corresponds to one of four elements and is required to unlock Vaults of the same element. They can also be sold for significant gold and have star-quality versions usable as home decor.

Pyroflow — Red
Dropped from Pyroflow-element Jaakcats and Sernuk. The most aggressive masked creatures with frontal shields that charge at you.
Hydroflow — Blue
Dropped from Hydroflow-element Jaakcats and Sernuk. These creatures are shielded on their back and prone to fleeing.
Terraflow — Green
Dropped from Terraflow-element Jaakcats and Sernuk. They stay down until alerted, then create a 360-degree shield.
Aeroflow — Yellow
Dropped from Aeroflow-element Jaakcats and Sernuk. Their shield pulses on and off in a 360-degree sphere pattern.
🛡️ Bootleg Masks
Can't hunt? Zeki sells Bootleg versions of each Flow Mask in the Amber Echo Store — they function identically to regular masks.

🛡️ Masked Creatures

Since Echo is more present in the Royal Highlands, the creatures have evolved magical masks to protect themselves and combat the effects of Echo. All masked creatures summon a shield when engaged.

  • Shooting an unshielded part stuns the creature for a few seconds and breaks the shield
  • Shooting the shield breaks it but prevents you from shooting again for a couple seconds
  • Dispel Arrows are particularly effective — they bypass shields entirely

Mask Behaviors

Pyroflow (Red)
The most aggressive. Shield in front. Charges at you — getting hit blocks you from shooting for a couple seconds.
Hydroflow (Blue)
Shielded on their back. Prone to fleeing — chase them down or cut them off.
Terraflow (Green)
Stay down until alerted. Once alerted, create a 360-degree shield — shoot when the shield drops or use Dispel Arrows.
Aeroflow (Yellow)
Shield pulses on and off in a 360-degree sphere. Time your shots for when the shield is down.

New Creatures

Jaakcat 🐱

Mischievous tricksters and thieves. Named after Jaak-o-lanterns which were inspired by their antics. They're quick but don't burrow or hide — if they escape, they run off until they feel safe to rest. Their rabbit-like movements make them an exciting, if unpredictable, hunting target.

Royal Sernuk 🦌

A new variant once domesticated by the Enfields and left to wander. They've returned to the wild with a distinct appearance and are tougher than regular Sernuks, requiring more hits to take down — but they drop higher quality loot.

🏺 Relics & Artifacts

Artifacts in the Royal Highlands are all tied to the influential Enfield family — four main members, each with unique artifacts scattered throughout the world that reveal different aspects of their story, character, and history.

  • Most artifact pieces can be found in rummage piles and are tradeable at Trading Stations in the Central Stables
  • A few rare pieces come from the Echo Store or treasure chests and cannot be traded
  • Completing an artifact rewards Certificates of Merit for relic upgrades

Four Enfield Relics

🏇 The Long Ride — Community Event

🐎 Limited-Time Event: May 25 – June 1, 2026

The Long Ride is a limited-time community event that puts the spotlight on mounts, exploration, and Amber Echo collection — all rolled into one massive, community-wide collaboration.

  • Every meter you ride mounted and every Amber Echo you collect counts toward a shared goal
  • Your distance traveled on a mount gets multiplied by your Amber Echo collected: e.g., 30m × 20 Amber Echo = 600 points
  • Organize riding parties, explore casually, or go all-out on Amber Echo expeditions — every step counts
  • Once milestones are hit, all rewards are delivered to your News Inbox — every adventurer celebrates!
💡 Tip
The Mount Multiplier is the key metric — ride further AND collect more Amber Echo to maximize your contribution. Watch the in-game News Announcements and Official Palia Discord for progress updates.

🎨 Outfit Dye System

We know that players have been asking for more ways to express their personality, and dyes will give players exactly that. You can take a traditional wedding dress and give it a gothic theme or change your Haute Dawg's ketchup to mustard. The possibilities are endless and I can't wait to see what the community creates! — S6 Michael
  • Outfit pieces now have up to 4 separate aspects you can dye
  • Dyes use the same Palettes as makeup and hair — 4 palettes available by default, more unlockable via Tailor Tickets
  • You apply an entire Palette at once (not individual colors) — you must unlock the full Palette to use it
  • Palettes typically cost 25 Tailor Tickets each
  • Tailor Tickets can also be purchased: 25 for 100 Palia Coin or 200 for 800 Palia Coin
  • This system supports all default outfits and a selection of the most popular premium outfits in 0.202, with more outfits rolling out in future patches
